**Primary Language:** English (`en-US` / `en-AE`)  
**Geographic Focus:** United Arab Emirates (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Northern Emirates)  
**Brand:** Ask Mareena (Mareena Tessa Thomas)  

---

## 1. Overview & Strategy

The SEO strategy for Ask Mareena focuses on high-intent, authoritative editorial positioning around UAE business setup, corporate structuring, tax compliance, and residency. Rather than generic keyword stuffing, the metadata emphasizes trustworthiness, personal expertise (12+ years in the UAE), and concrete procedural clarity.

## 3. Structured Data (JSON-LD) Implementations

1. **Organization & Person Schema:** Embedded on the root layout and About page linking Mareena Tessa Thomas, her 12+ years UAE experience, social profiles, and consultancy domain.
2. **ProfessionalService Schema:** Outlines the six core services, consultation geographic area (`AE`), and service hours.
3. **FAQPage Schema:** Injected dynamically on `/faq` and the homepage preview with question/acceptedAnswer pairs for rich search snippet eligibility.
4. **Service Schema:** Attached to individual `/services/[slug]` pages specifying service type, provider, and scope.
