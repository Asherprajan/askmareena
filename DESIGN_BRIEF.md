# Ask Mareena — Design Brief

## 1. Project

**Brand:** Ask Mareena  
**Website:** askmareena.com  
**Platform:** Next.js  
**Design status:** New visual identity — do not inherit the existing website design.

This document defines the visual and UX direction for the new Ask Mareena website.

The file `ASK_MAREENA_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` is the authority for business facts, services, biography, audience and approved information.

This document is the authority for design direction.

---

# 2. Core Design Objective

Create a premium personal consultancy website that makes a visitor feel:

> "I am dealing with a knowledgeable person who can simplify a complicated UAE business journey."

The website should not feel like:

- a generic company-formation agency
- a government portal
- a legal-document website
- a generic SaaS landing page
- a stock-template business website
- an AI-generated website
- an overly luxurious website that sacrifices trust

The visual identity should combine:

**Personal authority + UAE business expertise + editorial sophistication + approachable human guidance.**

---

# 3. Brand Personality

The design should communicate five primary qualities.

### 01. Trustworthy

The visitor should immediately feel that the brand is credible and responsible.

Use:

- clear information
- strong typography
- structured layouts
- restrained visual effects
- transparent messaging
- deliberate spacing

Avoid:

- exaggerated claims
- fake statistics
- unnecessary badges
- excessive testimonials
- gimmicky effects

---

### 02. Personal

Ask Mareena is centered around Mareena Tessa Thomas.

The website should feel like visitors are getting access to a real consultant rather than an anonymous corporate service provider.

Photography should therefore be an important part of the visual identity.

Use Mareena's supplied portrait as a primary brand asset.

---

### 03. Premium

Premium should come from:

- typography
- composition
- whitespace
- photography
- detail
- consistency
- interaction quality

Not from:

- excessive gold
- excessive gradients
- unnecessary glassmorphism
- oversized shadows
- decorative effects everywhere

---

### 04. Clear

The website deals with potentially complicated business topics.

The design should make information easy to understand.

Use:

- short content blocks
- clear hierarchy
- descriptive headings
- structured service information
- progressive disclosure
- clear CTAs

Do not overwhelm visitors with walls of text.

---

### 05. Human

The tone should remain warm and approachable.

The visual system should balance professional authority with personal warmth.

The visitor should not feel like they are filling out paperwork.

---

# 4. Visual Direction

## Recommended Direction

### "Editorial UAE Business Consultancy"

Combine the visual discipline of a premium editorial publication with the clarity of a modern consultancy.

The interface should feel:

- sophisticated
- calm
- intentional
- contemporary
- spacious
- image-led
- typography-led

The design should have enough character to be memorable without becoming decorative.

---

# 5. Design Independence Rule

The old Ask Mareena website must NOT be used as a visual reference.

Do not reuse or imitate:

- its CSS
- its color palette
- its typography
- its layouts
- its card treatment
- its spacing
- its navigation styling
- its gradients
- its visual hierarchy
- its animation patterns

The supplied website is a content/reference source only.

The new design must be independently conceived.

---

# 6. Color Direction

Create a sophisticated neutral-led palette.

The final implementation should use a restrained number of colors.

Recommended conceptual structure:

### Primary

A deep, sophisticated neutral for major surfaces and/or typography.

### Secondary

A warm neutral that creates human warmth.

### Accent

One distinctive accent associated with premium UAE positioning.

The accent must be used sparingly.

### Supporting

- soft background neutral
- primary text
- secondary text
- border
- muted text
- success
- error

Do not build the design around a large rainbow-like palette.

Do not rely on gradients as the primary visual identity.

---

# 7. Typography

Typography should be one of the strongest elements of the brand.

Use a sophisticated pairing such as:

### Display / Editorial

A high-quality serif or editorial display typeface.

Used for:

- hero headlines
- major section headings
- editorial statements
- important brand moments

### Interface / Body

A highly legible modern sans-serif.

Used for:

- body copy
- navigation
- buttons
- labels
- forms
- service details

Typography must create hierarchy without requiring decorative elements.

Avoid excessive font weights and too many typefaces.

Recommended hierarchy:

```text
Display
H1
H2
H3
Lead
Body
Small
Eyebrow / Label
```

---

# 8. Layout Philosophy

Use a strong editorial grid.

The website should feel composed rather than boxed into repetitive cards.

Prefer:

- asymmetric layouts
- large visual areas
- intentional whitespace
- overlapping sections when appropriate
- strong horizontal rhythm
- large headlines
- image/text compositions

Avoid:

- every section being a 3-column card grid
- identical section structures
- excessive containers
- excessive rounded cards
- visual repetition

---

# 9. Navigation

The navigation should be simple and premium.

Primary navigation:

- Home
- About
- Services
- FAQ
- Contact

Primary CTA:

**Ask Mareena**

The navigation should remain easy to use without occupying excessive screen space.

Desktop:

- clean horizontal navigation
- strong visual hierarchy
- clear CTA

Mobile:

- intentional mobile menu
- accessible keyboard/focus behavior
- clear CTA
- smooth but subtle transition

Do not over-design the navigation.

---

# 10. Homepage Experience

The homepage should tell a story.

Recommended sequence:

### 01 — Introduction

Immediately establish:

- what Ask Mareena does
- who Mareena helps
- why the visitor should continue

The hero should not contain unnecessary information.

---

### 02 — Credibility

Introduce relevant evidence from the approved source content.

Do not invent statistics or credentials.

---

### 03 — Mareena

Introduce the person behind the brand.

Use strong photography and concise storytelling.

The goal is to create trust before presenting too much service information.

---

### 04 — Services

Present the six core service categories clearly.

The service presentation should feel curated rather than like a directory.

Each service should have:

- number or visual identifier
- title
- concise description
- discover/read-more interaction

---

### 05 — Approach

Explain how Mareena helps clients.

Focus on clarity, practical support and guidance using approved content.

---

### 06 — Process

Create a simple process visualization.

Recommended conceptual structure:

```text
Understand
   ↓
Structure
   ↓
Establish
   ↓
Support
```

Do not imply guaranteed outcomes or timelines.

---

### 07 — FAQ

Answer important visitor questions in a clean accordion or editorial format.

Keep the interaction accessible.

---

### 08 — Conversion

Finish with a confident but non-aggressive invitation to start a conversation.

Example direction:

**Tell me what you want to build.**

The exact copy should be taken from or approved against the source-of-truth content.

---

# 11. About Page

The About page should feel more editorial than corporate.

Use:

- large portrait
- personal story
- short sections
- timeline or experience treatment where factually supported
- pull quotes only from approved content
- generous whitespace

The page should communicate the person behind the service.

Avoid:

- corporate mission statements
- generic leadership language
- fake awards
- invented credentials

---

# 12. Services Experience

The Services landing page should provide a clear overview.

The six service categories are:

1. Company Formation
2. Corporate Structuring
3. Tax & Accounting
4. Compliance
5. Visas & Residency
6. Ongoing Corporate Services

Each service should have its own detail page.

The design should make the service hierarchy obvious.

Use visual differentiation through:

- typography
- spacing
- numbering
- imagery
- layout

rather than assigning a different bright color to every service.

---

# 13. Service Detail Page

Each service detail page should feel like a premium advisory page.

Suggested structure:

```text
Service introduction
        ↓
What this covers
        ↓
Relevant areas
        ↓
Who this may be for
        ↓
How the engagement can begin
        ↓
Important considerations
        ↓
FAQ
        ↓
Contact CTA
```

Do not make unsupported promises.

Do not present government approval as guaranteed.

Do not invent prices or timelines.

---

# 14. Contact Experience

The contact page should reduce friction.

The visitor should understand:

1. what they can ask about
2. what information is needed
3. what happens after submission

The form should feel approachable rather than bureaucratic.

Required interaction states:

- default
- focus
- validation error
- submitting
- success
- server error

Use clear labels.

Do not rely on placeholder text as the only field label.

---

# 15. CTA System

There should be one primary CTA throughout the website.

Primary CTA concept:

**Ask Mareena**

Secondary CTA concepts may include:

- Explore Services
- Learn More
- Meet Mareena
- Start a Conversation

Avoid aggressive sales language.

CTA design should be consistent across:

- navigation
- hero
- service pages
- About
- footer
- contact

---

# 16. Cards

Cards should be used selectively.

Use cards when they improve:

- grouping
- comparison
- scanning
- interaction

Do not put every piece of content inside a card.

Prefer editorial sections for major storytelling.

Cards should have:

- clear hierarchy
- subtle borders or separation
- restrained radius
- minimal shadow
- strong spacing

Avoid excessive "floating card" aesthetics.

---

# 17. Photography

Photography is central to the personal brand.

Use the supplied Mareena portrait as a primary image.

Image treatment should feel:

- editorial
- natural
- premium
- authentic

Avoid:

- excessive filters
- artificial color grading
- heavy overlays
- unnecessary clipping
- generic stock-photo replacement for Mareena

If additional imagery is introduced later, it should support UAE business context without looking like generic stock photography.

---

# 18. Iconography

Use a single consistent icon family.

Icons should be:

- minimal
- thin or medium weight
- functional
- secondary to typography

Do not use icons as decoration everywhere.

Avoid mixing multiple icon styles.

---

# 19. Motion Design

Motion should be subtle and purposeful.

Use animation for:

- page entrance
- section reveal
- image reveal
- navigation transitions
- hover feedback
- accordion interaction
- CTA interaction

Avoid:

- constant movement
- large parallax effects
- excessive bouncing
- long animations
- distracting scroll effects

Animation duration should generally feel fast and refined.

Support:

`prefers-reduced-motion`

---

# 20. Mobile Design

Mobile is not a reduced desktop version.

Priorities:

1. Readability
2. Navigation
3. CTA visibility
4. Photography
5. Form usability
6. Touch targets
7. Performance

Ensure:

- comfortable typography
- adequate spacing
- no horizontal overflow
- properly cropped images
- accessible navigation
- easy-to-use forms
- appropriately sized buttons

Test at:

- 320px
- 375px
- 390px
- 430px

---

# 21. Accessibility

Target strong WCAG-aligned accessibility.

Include:

- semantic HTML
- keyboard navigation
- visible focus
- sufficient contrast
- accessible form labels
- descriptive links
- meaningful image alt text
- reduced-motion support
- logical heading hierarchy
- accessible accordions
- accessible mobile navigation

Accessibility must be designed into the components, not added at the end.

---

# 22. Responsive Layout

The system should use fluid layouts.

Avoid designing only around:

- 1440px
- 1920px

Use responsive containers and fluid typography.

Large screens should feel spacious.

Small screens should feel deliberate.

---

# 23. Design System Components

Create reusable components for:

### Global

- Header
- Mobile navigation
- Footer
- Container
- Section
- Button
- Link

### Content

- Section heading
- Service card
- Service list
- FAQ accordion
- Editorial block
- Image/text split
- Quote/pullout
- Process step
- CTA section

### Forms

- Input
- Select
- Textarea
- Form field
- Validation message
- Success state
- Error state

---

# 24. Design Tokens

Create centralized design tokens for:

- colors
- typography
- spacing
- radius
- shadows
- container widths
- breakpoints
- animation duration
- animation easing

Do not scatter arbitrary values throughout components.

---

# 25. Performance Principles

The design must not compromise performance.

Use:

- next/image
- responsive image sizes
- lazy loading where appropriate
- optimized fonts
- minimal client-side JavaScript
- Server Components by default
- dynamic imports only where useful

Animations should not create unnecessary performance costs.

---

# 26. SEO-Friendly Design

Design must support:

- one clear H1 per page
- logical H2/H3 hierarchy
- descriptive links
- crawlable navigation
- readable content
- internal linking
- service-specific URLs
- FAQ content
- structured data where appropriate

SEO should never make the UI feel spammy.

---

# 27. What NOT To Do

Do not create:

- generic SaaS hero sections
- excessive gradients
- excessive glassmorphism
- excessive rounded rectangles
- giant decorative blobs
- neon colors
- excessive gold luxury styling
- fake trust badges
- fake client logos
- fake testimonials
- invented awards
- invented statistics
- stock corporate people replacing Mareena
- excessive animations
- overly dense dashboards
- cookie-cutter three-card sections everywhere

Do not make the website look like a template.

---

# 28. Quality Benchmark

Before considering the design complete, ask:

### Brand

Does this feel like a personal expert brand?

### Trust

Does it feel credible without exaggeration?

### Premium

Does it feel premium because of craft rather than decoration?

### Clarity

Can a first-time visitor understand what Ask Mareena does quickly?

### Personality

Can the visitor remember Mareena rather than only the service list?

### Conversion

Is it obvious how to start a conversation?

### Mobile

Does the experience remain premium on a phone?

### Originality

Could someone mistake this for a generic template?

If yes, redesign the affected section.

---

# 29. Final Design Principle

The website should feel like:

**A trusted conversation with an experienced UAE business consultant, presented through a refined editorial digital experience.**

Not:

**A catalogue of company-formation services.**

The design should make Mareena the human connection between the visitor and the complex UAE business setup journey.
